CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The analysis reveals significant price variations across time, geography, and
commodity types. Strong inflationary trends are evident, with prices increasing
6054.0% over the study period. High volatility in certain 
commodities indicates market instability and potential food security risks.

Geographic disparities are extreme, though partly attributable to currency
differences. Seasonal effects exist but are moderate. These findings support
the need for continued price monitoring and targeted interventions in high-risk
markets and commodities.
